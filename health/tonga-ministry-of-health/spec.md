# spec.md — `leaders.yml`

Single source of truth for the Tongan health system register. The YAML must conform to this spec.

## 1. Purpose

Engagement register of named individuals in and around the Tonga Ministry of Health (MoH) and adjacent bodies.

The Kingdom of Tonga is a constitutional monarchy under King Tupou VI. The Hu'akavameiliku Siaosi Sovaleni was Prime Minister; political dynamics shifted in late 2024 with Sovaleni's resignation and Aisake Eke becoming Prime Minister. PIF, ACP, Commonwealth member.

## 2. Scope

In scope: King; Prime Minister; Minister of Health; CEO Vaiola Hospital; Legislative Assembly.

Out of scope: district health officers.
