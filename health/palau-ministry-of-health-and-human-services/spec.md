Single source of truth for the Palauan health system register. The YAML must conform to this spec.

## 1. Purpose

Engagement register of named individuals in and around the Republic of Palau Ministry of Health and Human Services and adjacent bodies. Palau operates under the Compact of Free Association (COFA) with the US — renewed in 2024. PIF, ACP, PSIDS member; maintains diplomatic relations with Taiwan (one of the few states to do so).

## 2. Scope

In scope: President; Minister of Health and Human Services; Belau National Hospital; Olbiil Era Kelulau (National Congress).

Out of scope: state health office staff.
