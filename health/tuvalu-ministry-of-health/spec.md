## 1. Purpose

Engagement register of named individuals in and around the Tuvalu Ministry of Health, Social Welfare and Gender Affairs and adjacent bodies. Tuvalu is one of the smallest sovereign states (~12,000 population, ~26 km²) and a Commonwealth realm under King Charles III. PIF, ACP, Commonwealth, PSIDS member; maintains diplomatic relations with Taiwan.

## 2. Scope
