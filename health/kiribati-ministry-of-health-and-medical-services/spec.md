# spec.md — `leaders.yml`

Single source of truth for the i-Kiribati health system register. The YAML must conform to this spec.

## 1. Purpose

Engagement register of named individuals in and around the Republic of Kiribati Ministry of Health and Medical Services (MHMS) and adjacent bodies. Kiribati is the only state straddling all four hemispheres (across the equator and the International Date Line). PIF, ACP, Commonwealth, PSIDS member.

## 2. Scope

In scope: Beretitenti (President); Minister of Health and Medical Services; Tungaru Central Hospital; Maneaba ni Maungatabu (Parliament).

Out of scope: outer-island health officers.

## 3. Structure

Standard 2/6/10/14 indentation.

## 4. Field definitions

Party affiliation: `TKP` (Tobwaan Kiribati Party, ruling under President Taneti Maamau), `BKM` (Boutokaan Kiribati Moa, opposition). Titles in English.
