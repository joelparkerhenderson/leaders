## 1. Purpose

Engagement register of named individuals in and around the Republic of Nauru Department of Health and Medical Services and adjacent bodies. Nauru is the world's third-smallest sovereign state by area (~21 km²) and population (~12,000). PIF, ACP, Commonwealth, PSIDS member.

## 2. Scope

In scope: President; Minister for Health; Director Republic of Nauru Hospital; Parliament.

Out of scope: clinic-level staff.
