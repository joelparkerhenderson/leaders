# spec.md — `leaders.yml`

Single source of truth for the Federated States of Micronesia (FSM) health system register. The YAML must conform to this spec.

## 1. Purpose

Engagement register of named individuals in and around the FSM Department of Health and Social Affairs (DHSA) and the four State health-administrations (Pohnpei, Chuuk, Kosrae, Yap). FSM operates under the Compact of Free Association (COFA) with the US — renewed in 2024. PIF, PSIDS, ACP member.

## 2. Scope

In scope: President; Secretary DHSA; State Governors and State Department of Health Services leaders; Congress.

Out of scope: outer-island dispensary staff.

## 8. Anti-patterns

- Treating FSM as unitary — four states (Pohnpei, Chuuk, Kosrae, Yap) operate distinct health-administrations with significant autonomy under the FSM Constitution.
- Ignoring COFA.
