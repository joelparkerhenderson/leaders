# spec.md — `leaders.yml`

Single source of truth for the Marshall Islands health system register. The YAML must conform to this spec.

## 1. Purpose

Engagement register of named individuals in and around the Republic of the Marshall Islands (RMI) Ministry of Health and Human Services and adjacent bodies. RMI operates under the Compact of Free Association (COFA) with the United States — renewed in 2024 — which provides significant financing and US medical-system access for citizens. PIF and PSIDS member; legacy nuclear-testing health programmes.

## 2. Scope

In scope: President; Minister of Health and Human Services; Director Public Health; Majuro Hospital and Ebeye Hospital; Nitijela (Parliament).

Out of scope: outer-atoll dispensary staff.
